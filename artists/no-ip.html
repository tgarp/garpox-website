01Artist introduction

no_ip is a Barcelona-based electronic music producer whose sound moves between house, electro, indie dance and atmospheric breaks.

Drawing on more than a decade of musical experimentation, he has developed a distinctive approach that combines groove-driven rhythms, electronic textures and melodic elements.

His releases on labels such as DSK Records, Get Physical Music and Clash Lion reflect his versatility and interest in exploring different sides of underground electronic music.

02Sound & musical identity

What drew me to no_ip was his completely different sonic identity. The dreamy sounds on his album Block The System impressed me with their fresh approach to electronic music.

This alternative approach creates his unique sound and makes him stand out among electronic artists in 2026. His single ‘Starshine’ made me dive deeper into his catalogue and follow the journey.

03Artist positioning

What is interesting about no_ip is his ability to combine genres and incorporate his unique sound into them.

Hearing his track on Pete Tong's radio show gave me the motivation to listen to more of his music. It was encouraging to see that an artist doesn't need millions of monthly listeners to get radio support.

04My A&R perspective

It has been fascinating to follow no_ip over the past years, from his first few releases in 2020 to his still experimental approach in 2026.

no_ip has been in the industry for over a decade and is a DJ with his finger on the pulse of the industry. I'll be keeping an eye on his sonic development, which is what makes his productions unique, and I'm curious to see it carried into future collaborations.

I am sure no_ip will have a place in the industry for many years to come, and my recommendation is to keep following his journey.

05Why I selected no_ip

I discovered no_ip by chance through Pete Tong's show on BBC Radio 1. The track stayed with me long after, and I wanted to share this excitement here in my Artist Discovery.

With more than a decade of experience behind him, I think he is well placed to develop his sound further over the next years, for everyone to discover.
