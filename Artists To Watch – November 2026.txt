Artists To Watch – November 2026 
Artist: SYREETA 
Based in: UK 
Genre: House / Techno 
Discovered through: Sarah Story — BBC Radio 1, Future Dance 

Artist introduction 
SYREETA is a British DJ and producer whose sound moves between house and techno, 
combining bass-driven grooves with melodic elements. With an established presence in the 
international club scene, she has increasingly developed her identity as a producer through 
original releases, remixes and collaborations. 
Her musical identity is particularly interesting because she brings together different sides of 
electronic music, rather than limiting herself to one specific subgenre. 
Her releases have appeared on labels including Nervous Records, Edible and 
HE.SHE.THEY., while her DJ career includes appearances at major clubs and festivals 
internationally.  

Sound & musical identity 
What initially caught my attention about SYREETA was the balance between groove, energy 
and melodic elements in her music. 
Her sound has a strong club-oriented foundation, but it is not limited to straightforward 
dancefloor functionality. The combination of chunky basslines and melodic grooves gives her 
productions and DJ sets a distinctive character. 
I find the relationship between house and techno particularly interesting in her musical 
identity. It allows her to move between different club environments while maintaining a 
recognisable musical direction.

Artist positioning 
One of the most interesting aspects of SYREETA's career is the development of her identity 
from an experienced DJ into an increasingly recognised recording artist. 
Her DJ career has provided her with an established position within the international house 
and techno scene. Her production work offers another dimension to that identity, allowing 
audiences to connect with her music beyond her DJ sets. 
I think this is an interesting example of how an artist can use an existing position within club 
culture as a foundation for developing a stronger identity as a producer. 


My A&R perspective 
What makes SYREETA interesting to me is the way she combines an established 
understanding of club culture with her own musical productions. 
I would be interested in exploring how she can continue developing a recognisable 
production identity while maintaining the versatility that makes her DJ sets distinctive. 
I also think her ability to move between different areas of house and techno gives her 
opportunities to reach different audiences without necessarily compromising her musical 
identity. 
What I would keep an eye on the relationship between her DJ identity, original productions 
and future collaborations, particularly how these elements contribute to a more recognisable 
artistic direction. 

Why I selected SYREETA 
I discovered SYREETA through Sarah Story's Future Dance show on BBC Radio 1. Her 
music caught my attention because of its combination of groove, energy and melodic 
elements. 
I wanted to include her in my Artist Discovery because I find the development of her 
production career particularly interesting, especially in relation to her already established DJ 
background. 


Artist: no_ip 
Based in: Spain 
Genre: Indie Tech / Electronic 
Discovered through: Pete Tong — BBC Radio 1 
Artist Introduction 
no_ip is a Barcelona-based electronic music producer whose sound moves between house, 
electro, indie dance and atmospheric breaks. With a background in over a decade of musical 
experimentation, he has developed a distinctive approach that combines groove-driven 
rhythms, electronic textures and melodic elements. His releases on labels such as DSK 
Records, Get Physical Music and Clash Lion reflect his versatility and interest in exploring 
different sides of underground electronic music. 
Sound & musical identity 
What made me discover ni_ip was his totally different sound identity. The dreamy sounds on 
his album Block The System made me very impressed on his different approach on electronic 
music.  
The alternative approach creates his unique sound, and makes him significantly stand out 
from other electronic artists in 2026. His single ‘Starshine’ really made me dive into his 
catalogue and experience the journey. 


TRACKS TO EXPLORE 
• Starshine 
• Social Arcade 
• Like This 

Artist positioning 
What is interesting about no_ip is his ability to combine genres and incorporate his unique 
sound into it.  
Experiencing his track on Pete Tongs radio show, it gave me a motivation to listen to more of 
his music. I found it promising to see that DJs do not necessarily need millions of monthly 
listeners to still get radio recognition. 

 
My A&R perspective 
It is super interesting to have followed no_ip over the past years. From his first few releases 
in 2020 to now in 2026, with his still experimental approach. 
No_ip has been in the industry for over a decade and is a DJ with a pulse on what is going on 
in the industry. I am going to keep an eye on his sonic development, which makes his 
productions unique, and I would be very curious to see this happening in future 
collaborations as well. 
I am sure no_ip will have a place in the industry in the next many years, and it is my 
recommendation to follow his path. 


Why I selected no_ip 
I discovered no_ip occasionally through Pete Tong’s show on BBC Radio 1. His song had a

With several years of experience, I think he will develop his sound identity over the next
