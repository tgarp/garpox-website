01Artist introduction

SYREETA is a British DJ and producer whose sound moves between house and techno, combining bass-driven grooves with melodic elements.

With an established presence in the international club scene, she has increasingly developed her identity as a producer through original releases, remixes and collaborations.

What stands out about her musical identity is that she brings together different sides of electronic music, rather than limiting herself to one specific subgenre.

Her releases have appeared on labels including Nervous Records, Edible and HE.SHE.THEY., while her DJ career has taken her to major clubs and festivals around the world.

02Sound & musical identity

What initially caught my attention about SYREETA was the balance between groove, energy and melodic elements in her music.

Her sound has a strong club-oriented foundation, but it goes beyond pure dancefloor functionality. The combination of chunky basslines and melodic grooves gives her productions and DJ sets a distinctive character.

The interplay between house and techno is central to her musical identity. It allows her to move between different club environments while maintaining a recognisable musical direction.

03Artist positioning

One of the most compelling aspects of SYREETA's career is the development of her identity from an experienced DJ into an increasingly recognised recording artist.

Her DJ career has given her an established place in the international house and techno scene. Her production work offers another dimension to that identity, allowing audiences to connect with her music beyond her DJ sets.

It's a strong example of how an artist can use an existing position within club culture as a foundation for developing a stronger identity as a producer.

04My A&R perspective

What makes SYREETA stand out to me is the way she combines an established understanding of club culture with her own musical productions.

I'd be curious to see how she continues to develop a recognisable production identity while maintaining the versatility that makes her DJ sets distinctive.

I also think her ability to move between different areas of house and techno lets her reach different audiences without compromising her sound.

What I would keep an eye on is the relationship between her DJ identity, original productions and future collaborations — particularly how these elements contribute to a more recognisable artistic direction.

05Why I selected SYREETA

I discovered SYREETA through Sarah Story's Future Dance show on BBC Radio 1. Her music caught my attention because of its combination of groove, energy and melodic elements.

I wanted to include her in my Artist Discovery because I find the growth of her production career exciting, especially alongside her already established DJ background.
