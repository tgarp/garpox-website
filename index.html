About me

Curious, creative and close to the music.

I'm a Creative Business student with a strong interest in electronic music and the creative side of the music industry.

My interests lie particularly in A&R, artist development and music marketing, with a focus on discovering emerging talent and understanding what makes an artist stand out.

With a particular passion for house, melodic electronic music, UK garage and other electronic subgenres, I enjoy exploring new sounds, following emerging artists and developing my own perspectives on music and artist identities.

Alongside my studies, I've gained practical experience through my own artist project, where I develop and create short-form content for Instagram and TikTok. I've also gained experience in radio and live music environments, further strengthening my interest in the industry.

I'm curious, creative and motivated by the opportunity to contribute to the development of artists and their music.
